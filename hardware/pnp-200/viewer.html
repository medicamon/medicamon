Key numbers

Frame460 × 480 mm (562 mm wide over the X drive), 352 mm tall to the Z motors; 2040/2020 extrusion
Bed (Y)140 × 186 mm plate, 200 mm travel, 2 × MGN12 rails, 4 × MGN12H blocks
Board100 × 80 mm pocket on the front half of the bed, board top 96 mm above the table
Head (X)230 mm travel, 1 × MGN12H on the gantry beam
Nozzle (Z1, C)40 mm Z. C: OUKEDA OK28ZK34 hollow-shaft NEMA 11 (35.6 mm measured), rotary push-in fitting for 4 mm hose, Juki-type holder with a Juki 500-series nozzle
Syringe (Z2)40 mm Z. 10 cc barrel; the plunger is pushed by an AFDE 42SH3402Y NEMA 17 linear stepper (screw through the motor)
X/Y driveNEMA 17 0.9° (Lin WO-4209L-01P), GT2 belt, 20T pulley: 0.1 mm per full step, 160 microsteps/mm at 1/16
Z drive (each head)Lin WO-4209L-01P (0.9°) → 5 × 8 coupler → T8 screw in 2 × KP08, anti-backlash nut: 0.02 mm per full step with an 8 mm lead. Guide: Ø8 rod in 2 × SK8 with an SCS8UU
Feeders6 strips of 8 mm tape across the back half of the bed, 4 mm sprocket pitch
VacuumPump and valve on the outside of the right post; the hose loops over the gantry to the nozzle
VisionDown camera on the head (fiducials), up camera on the frame (part alignment)
Tool offsetsNozzle +40 mm, syringe −40 mm, camera 0 in X

Concept model for layout and clearances. Rail, motor and pulley sizes follow HIWIN MGN and NEMA catalogue values; check the datasheets for the parts you buy before machining.
